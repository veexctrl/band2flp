# Unknowns and required evidence

- Semantic field layouts and relationships for the observed chunk types inside the `DfLogicModelLogicSong` `NS.data` payload.
- Whether Logic Pro chunk decoders transfer unchanged to iOS GarageBand versions.
- Scope and semantics of the 32-bit header value at offset 8: it is reused by `TxSt` and audio chunk families, so it is not globally unique. Its role in source/region grouping needs validation with controlled projects and edits.
- Whether `AuRg +0x16` is source-frame length for all GarageBand versions versus another cached length. Its values are preserved as candidates, not used as timeline duration.
- ARR-020 compared `AuRg +0x16` against embedded source frame counts: only a subset of candidates in one project matched. The field is not a universal full-source length; trimming and region-length semantics remain unknown.
- Exact beat duration/source-offset/trim/loop fields in `AuRg`, exact mapping from each placement event to a specific same-source region object, and meaning of the extra 80-byte suffix on placement events. ARR-023 found suffix-to-`AuRg +0x16` candidate matches in two fixtures, but not for every extended event; ARR-032 found that two of three fixed-field candidates also appear among ambiguous suffix candidates. Starts and track/source placement links remain the only complete arrangement fields recovered with HIGH CONFIDENCE for the preview fixture.
- Meaning of the repeatable changed-byte pattern between same-source `AuRg` payloads sharing `+0x16` candidates. ARR-034 found two equal-sized pairs with 27/28 changed offsets and 26 common offsets; IDA matched all four payloads byte-for-byte. These may be instance or edit fields, but the uncontrolled fixture does not establish their semantics.
- Whether the `AuRg +0x8a` candidate is a byte-sized, zero-based region index or a stable instance key stored in an eight-byte slot. ARR-035 found only the first byte varying across four repeated-frame records. ARR-036 linked all four records to placements in one fixture, but repeated zero fields in the second fixture did not link; a controlled reorder/copy test is still needed to distinguish current serialized order from stable identity.
- ARR-019's earlier nonzero/sentinel histogram was not reproducible. IDA and the parser both read zero at `0x24 +0x18` for the nine records in chunk 298; the field remains UNKNOWN and is not used for region length.
- Meaning of the little-endian 32-bit word at audio placement `+0x1c`. ARR-027 found finite values on the candidate 960-tick grid in two fixtures and one preview-length match, but most records carry `0x3fffffff`; finite-word duration semantics and sentinel meaning remain HYPOTHESIS/UNKNOWN. It is exposed only as a raw candidate and is not used to populate `duration_beats`. A controlled same-source, same-start region-length edit is required.
- Meaning of the repeated 16-byte `0xF1` `EvSq` payloads and the scope of their candidate group values. EVT-007 found byte identity within and across two payloads, exactly one occurrence of the record at each parsed `0xF1` event start, and IDA/Python agreement for all 68 event byte ranges and 204 source/chunk group fields across both fixtures. EVT-008 found exact serialized group-sequence equality with `MSeq` and empty `Trak` chunks in both fixtures; the aligned sequences each have three adjacent decreases, so numeric sorting does not explain them. This supports an ordinal structural correspondence but does not assign object identity, an event role, or group semantics.
- Tempo-event position units, meter pre-roll semantics, scope of nonzero-group tempo records, and whether the decoded event set is a complete tempo map. EVT-009 found a nonzero tempo candidate co-located with the placement stream in both fixtures, but its position matched no parsed placement start and the one available CAF-derived source tempo matched neither it nor the summary; this does not distinguish local tempo, template state, or another role.
- Track names, complete track ordering (including the unpopulated track slot), mixer settings, and mute/solo state. Audio placement track numbers 2 through 7 are recovered for this fixture. PD-002/PD-003 confirmed that the top-level arrange-model archive branch in both supplied fixtures contains editor/UI state rather than a track/region collection. TRK-009 maps the previously selected track UUID to one `Trak` chunk in each fixture; a complete UUID-to-arrange-track mapping and ordering are still unavailable. CROSS-004 found audio filename stems in `SngO` and `GenM` strings, but their distinct source groups and unknown chunk semantics do not identify arrange tracks.
- Whether `MSeq +0x12` strings are displayed track names, sequence/region labels, or instrument labels. MIDI-016 found exact matches to three visible arrangement labels in one private screenshot; all three matched strings belong to unique MIDI-linked `MSeq` chunks with note candidates. This supports a limited relationship but cannot distinguish name scope or generalize to all tracks.
- Whether other `Trak +0x18` UUIDs are referenced outside the logic-song payload through non-plist components or encodings not covered by TRK-011. Only the saved selected-track UUID matched the inspected keyed-archive strings; companion plist components yielded no matches.
- Whether track UUID fields are regenerated with track edits or referenced through indirect/encoded relationships. TRK-012 found no additional direct binary or text occurrence of those UUIDs within the two logic-song chunk streams.
- ARR-021 corroborated grouping and start positions against six preview rows, but numeric track labels are absent from the image; a controlled reorder/add-track experiment is still needed to confirm absolute track indices.
- Unit and completeness semantics of `com_apple_garageband_metadata_songDuration`. META-002 found that neither the last recovered audio start nor a finite `+0x1c`-based end (under a 960-PPQ hypothesis) directly matches the summary in the two archives; incomplete audio durations and missing MIDI/end-tail placement prevent stronger conclusions.
- Which `AuCO` records, if any, correspond to arrange tracks, mixer channels, auxiliary channels, or other channels. TRK-004 falsified a one-to-one count mapping in two supplied projects (23 candidates versus 7 arrange tracks; 27 versus 12); controlled track-count fixtures are still needed to identify any subset.
- Meaning of `Trak` chunk-header offset `+0x0E`. It is `0xFFFF` across 129 empty and 58-byte `Trak` records in two inspected payloads (TRK-005); whether this is an unset sentinel, reserved value, or another constant remains unknown until controlled track edits are compared.
- Meaning of the varying first-eight-byte patterns in 58-byte `Trak` payloads. TRK-006 found three variants in one payload and 13 in another; only the first two bytes were common within each. TRK-009 found unique version-1 UUID-shaped values at `+0x18` in all 61 observed 58-byte `Trak` payloads, but whether each record is an arrange track or another track-related object remains unknown.
- Meaning/scope of group values on 58-byte `Trak` chunks. TRK-010 found that group equality can collect many `Trak` records with the audio placement-event stream, so it is not a one-to-one track join; any group-scoped role remains unknown.
- Meaning of the exact group-multiplicity match between all `MSeq` and empty-payload `Trak` chunks (TRK-007). The match repeats across two payloads but does not establish whether an empty `Trak` is a track, placeholder, or another member of a larger serialized group.
- Whether MIDI placement `+0x14` identifies a track or an entry in another table. TRK-008 found no shared-group link from recognized MIDI placements to validated `AuCO` candidates. TRK-010's `AuCO` ordinal comparison was not discriminative, and TRK-013 found no consistent zero- or one-based mapping to linked `MSeq` or empty-`Trak` serialized ordinals. MIDI-017 found consecutive values for three screenshot-ordered MIDI rows, but other values exceed the declared track count. Treat this as a relative-order hypothesis only; a controlled moved-track fixture is needed to test identity.
- Meaning of the eight-byte `AuCO` descriptor for GarageBand and how arrange-track records can be distinguished from non-arrangement channel strips.
- Region source offsets, trims, loops, and mute state. ARR-024 visually measures five preview clip lengths in one fixture, but the serialized duration field remains unknown; decoded placement starts are in beats.
- Whether the Logic-derived offsets on 80-byte GarageBand `0x90` events mean pitch, velocity, onset, and duration; EVT-006 found variation at several candidate offsets among four records but cannot attribute it to musical properties. Candidate fields remain unconfirmed pending controlled one-note GarageBand fixtures.
- Whether the shared-`MSeq` relation between `0x90` note-shaped events and `0x20` MIDI placements generalizes beyond the private fixture; MIDI-006 observed one unique shared chunk/placement link per note event there, but controlled note edits are still needed.
- Whether the Logic-derived `+0x0b` and `+0x0c` offsets in `0x91`–`0x9e` records encode velocity and pitch. EVT-010 found MIDI-domain values in all 129 filtered records, but no controlled edit links either offset to a note property. Their `+0x1c` words are all nonzero and none is on a 960-tick grid, leaving duration units unknown.
- Whether GarageBand note positions are region-relative. MIDI-007 found that this interpretation avoids placing all four linked note candidates before their unique region placement under Logic-derived origins; this is a fixture-specific HYPOTHESIS because neither GarageBand's position origin nor placement semantics have been controlled.
- Whether the `MSeq` payload's `+0x11c` word represents a region start in GarageBand. MIDI-008 found only zero-to-zero equality at that word; the two nonzero placement tick candidates did not match it. A nonzero controlled region-move fixture is required before assigning any timing meaning.
- Whether the event-sequence tempo/meter lists are complete or how their position fields are measured. EVT-003 confirms one group-zero tempo and meter candidate matching both summary plists in two projects, but does not test tempo/meter changes or other project variants.
- An independent Logic Pro 11.2.2 spec identifies `AuRg +0x16` as a region frame count there. ARR-025 found all same-source candidates at or below full-source frame counts in one GarageBand fixture, but ARR-024 did not match preview lengths under un-stretched playback. Whether iOS GarageBand retains the Logic meaning, and how frame lengths map through trim/loop/stretch to beats, remains UNKNOWN. See ARR-020, ARR-024, ARR-025, and [the Logic Pro format write-up](https://github.com/jonkubis/LogicProFormatWriter/blob/main/PROJECTDATA_FORMAT.md#8-audio-regions).
- GarageBand-specific `MSeq` record fields for MIDI region duration, internal start, and name. MIDI-015 tested Logic record-relative `+0x78` and `+0x11c` after subtracting the 36-byte chunk header; neither translated offset is supported by the observed values. Other layouts or offsets remain possible.
- MSeq payload structure beyond its common 8-byte prefix. MIDI-013 found no Standard MIDI `MThd` header in 68 payloads; exploratory tail-word positions show varied values and have no GarageBand field interpretation. MIDI-014 found 309-byte payloads for all placed groups without the observed `0x90`/`0x91`–`0x9e` events in two fixtures, while groups with those events in one fixture have other lengths; this is an uncontrolled correlation, and no length semantics are established.
- MIDI-005's linked-`MSeq` position scan was inconclusive because the fixture had too few distinct nonzero starts; no scanned payload offset is treated as region start. MIDI-008's `+0x11c` scan was payload-relative, not the Logic record-relative location; MIDI-015 corrects and tests that offset translation.
- MIDI `0x20` event `+0x14` track identity semantics and whether `+0x04` uses the Logic-derived 34,560/960 PPQ conversion in GarageBand. MIDI-009 rules out treating `+0x14` as a direct arrange-track index in the two inspected fixtures, but it may be an identifier or index into another object table. MIDI-010 found one zero-payload, same-group `Trak` chunk alongside every unique `MSeq` target; whether that group relation identifies an arrange track remains UNKNOWN. The `+0x20` cluster-to-`MSeq` association is HIGH CONFIDENCE in two fixtures; timing semantics still need controlled validation.
- MIDI-004's raw `Trak` chunk-order comparison did not establish a direct mapping for `0x20 +0x14`; repeated chunk groups make ordinal equality insufficient.
- Where the referenced live-loop audio is stored/resolved for this project; the supplied `.band` archive itself contains no audio payload members.
- Whether an `AudioFiles` reference originated as imported audio, an Apple Loop, or audio used by a Live Loops cell. AUD-006 found six external CAF references containing an `Apple Loops` path component in one fixture; this supports a library-origin interpretation but does not distinguish ordinary audio from Live Loops cell/grid semantics. Successfully resolving a file reference and placing its audio does not preserve the library classification; CAF metadata may also omit this information.
- Whether CAF beat-count metadata controls source tempo, region duration, or repeat spacing. AUD-005 linked one beat-tagged source to four placement candidates, but only one of the three adjacent candidate-start intervals was an exact integer multiple of that tag under the unvalidated placement timing conversion; neither ordinary audio versus Live Loops use nor trim behavior is controlled.
- Audio source references and region-to-media mapping.
- Tempo and time-signature changes, sections, automation, fades, and region gain.
- Whether output metadata and asset lists are complete, stale, or derived caches.
- Which structures differ across GarageBand versions and desktop/mobile projects.

- Semantics of unclassified EvSq event types. EVT-004 inventories aggregate counts and lengths for 50 type bytes outside the five event types with candidate decoders in one fixture, and IDA confirms one representative type byte for each. EVT-005 links all observed `0x91`–`0x9e` candidate groups in one audio-bearing fixture to unique `MSeq` and MIDI-placement candidates, but does not establish whether these records are notes, controllers, instrument state, or other region-scoped data.
- PROV-001 found GarageBand 2.3.19 in both inspected project archives. The fixtures have different contents and are not controlled variants, so version-, device-, or project-specific serialization effects cannot be isolated from this comparison.
- FLP-027 identified a concrete FLP ordering defect: PyFLP-appended audio channel definitions followed the playlist, while 25 local FL Studio references placed channel definitions before it. Moving five cloned blocks before the playlist, without changing their payloads or the clip records, let FL Studio 25 open the local v19 probe with visible clips. The exporter now performs this relocation and validates its event order. A fresh two-source exporter output also opened in FL Studio 25 with both silent synthetic clips visible. FLP-028 found all six referenced sample paths exist, but the private CAF source uses AAC at 44.1 kHz stereo. Image-Line's sample-format documentation does not list CAF, so unsupported CAF/AAC decoding is the likely cause of the missing-sample dialog. A local accessible CAF copy was transcoded to WAV, grafted into a playlist-preserving local FLP probe, and the user reports that FL Studio 25 loaded it fully. An in-memory hash comparison confirmed that the transcode input matched the sole CAF member in the supplied project archive byte-for-byte; playback remains unverified; no audio data was copied to the repository. GarageBand region durations also remain unknown. PyFLP 2.2.1 still does not itself verify the 80-byte FL Studio 25 playlist format; its runtime and licensing limitations remain.
