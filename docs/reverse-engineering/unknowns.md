# Unknowns and required evidence

- Semantic field layouts and relationships for the observed chunk types inside the `DfLogicModelLogicSong` `NS.data` payload.
- Whether Logic Pro chunk decoders transfer unchanged to iOS GarageBand versions.
- Scope and semantics of the 32-bit header value at offset 8: it is reused by `TxSt` and audio chunk families, so it is not globally unique. Its role in source/region grouping needs validation with controlled projects and edits.
- Whether `AuRg +0x16` is source-frame length for all GarageBand versions versus another cached length. Its values are preserved as candidates, not used as timeline duration.
- ARR-020 compared `AuRg +0x16` against embedded source frame counts: only a subset of candidates in one project matched. The field is not a universal full-source length; trimming and region-length semantics remain unknown.
- Exact beat duration/source-offset/trim/loop fields in `AuRg`, exact mapping from each placement event to a specific same-source region object, and meaning of the extra 80-byte suffix on placement events. ARR-023 found suffix-to-`AuRg +0x16` candidate matches in two fixtures, but not for every extended event; starts and track/source placement links remain the only complete arrangement fields recovered with HIGH CONFIDENCE for the preview fixture.
- ARR-019's earlier nonzero/sentinel histogram was not reproducible. IDA and the parser both read zero at `0x24 +0x18` for the nine records in chunk 298; the field remains UNKNOWN and is not used for region length.
- Meaning of the repeated 16-byte `0xF1` `EvSq` payloads and the scope of their candidate group values.
- Tempo-event position units, meter pre-roll semantics, scope of nonzero-group tempo records, and whether the decoded event set is a complete tempo map.
- Track names, complete track ordering (including the unpopulated track slot), mixer settings, and mute/solo state. Audio placement track numbers 2 through 7 are recovered for this fixture. PD-002/PD-003 confirmed that the top-level arrange-model archive branch in both supplied fixtures contains editor/UI state rather than a track/region collection. TRK-009 maps the previously selected track UUID to one `Trak` chunk in each fixture; a complete UUID-to-arrange-track mapping and ordering are still unavailable.
- Whether other `Trak +0x18` UUIDs are referenced outside the logic-song payload through non-plist components or encodings not covered by TRK-011. Only the saved selected-track UUID matched the inspected keyed-archive strings; companion plist components yielded no matches.
- ARR-021 corroborated grouping and start positions against six preview rows, but numeric track labels are absent from the image; a controlled reorder/add-track experiment is still needed to confirm absolute track indices.
- Which `AuCO` records, if any, correspond to arrange tracks, mixer channels, auxiliary channels, or other channels. TRK-004 falsified a one-to-one count mapping in two supplied projects (23 candidates versus 7 arrange tracks; 27 versus 12); controlled track-count fixtures are still needed to identify any subset.
- Meaning of `Trak` chunk-header offset `+0x0E`. It is `0xFFFF` across 129 empty and 58-byte `Trak` records in two inspected payloads (TRK-005); whether this is an unset sentinel, reserved value, or another constant remains unknown until controlled track edits are compared.
- Meaning of the varying first-eight-byte patterns in 58-byte `Trak` payloads. TRK-006 found three variants in one payload and 13 in another; only the first two bytes were common within each. TRK-009 found unique version-1 UUID-shaped values at `+0x18` in all 61 observed 58-byte `Trak` payloads, but whether each record is an arrange track or another track-related object remains unknown.
- Meaning/scope of group values on 58-byte `Trak` chunks. TRK-010 found that group equality can collect many `Trak` records with the audio placement-event stream, so it is not a one-to-one track join; any group-scoped role remains unknown.
- Meaning of the exact group-multiplicity match between all `MSeq` and empty-payload `Trak` chunks (TRK-007). The match repeats across two payloads but does not establish whether an empty `Trak` is a track, placeholder, or another member of a larger serialized group.
- Whether MIDI placement `+0x14` identifies a track or an entry in another table. TRK-008 found no shared-group link from recognized MIDI placements to validated `AuCO` candidates, but does not test indirect or ordinal relationships.
- Meaning of the eight-byte `AuCO` descriptor for GarageBand and how arrange-track records can be distinguished from non-arrangement channel strips.
- Region source offsets, trims, loops, and mute state. ARR-024 visually measures five preview clip lengths in one fixture, but the serialized duration field remains unknown; decoded placement starts are in beats.
- Whether the Logic-derived offsets on 80-byte GarageBand `0x90` events mean pitch, velocity, onset, and duration; candidate fields are exposed but remain unconfirmed pending controlled one-note GarageBand fixtures.
- Whether the shared-`MSeq` relation between `0x90` note-shaped events and `0x20` MIDI placements generalizes beyond the private fixture; MIDI-006 observed one unique shared chunk/placement link per note event there, but controlled note edits are still needed.
- Whether GarageBand note positions are region-relative. MIDI-007 found that this interpretation avoids placing all four linked note candidates before their unique region placement under Logic-derived origins; this is a fixture-specific HYPOTHESIS because neither GarageBand's position origin nor placement semantics have been controlled.
- Whether the `MSeq` payload's `+0x11c` word represents a region start in GarageBand. MIDI-008 found only zero-to-zero equality at that word; the two nonzero placement tick candidates did not match it. A nonzero controlled region-move fixture is required before assigning any timing meaning.
- Whether the event-sequence tempo/meter lists are complete or how their position fields are measured. EVT-003 confirms one group-zero tempo and meter candidate matching both summary plists in two projects, but does not test tempo/meter changes or other project variants.
- An independent Logic Pro 11.2.2 spec identifies `AuRg +0x16` as a region frame count there. ARR-025 found all same-source candidates at or below full-source frame counts in one GarageBand fixture, but ARR-024 did not match preview lengths under un-stretched playback. Whether iOS GarageBand retains the Logic meaning, and how frame lengths map through trim/loop/stretch to beats, remains UNKNOWN. See ARR-020, ARR-024, ARR-025, and [the Logic Pro format write-up](https://github.com/jonkubis/LogicProFormatWriter/blob/main/PROJECTDATA_FORMAT.md#8-audio-regions).
- GarageBand-specific `MSeq` record fields for MIDI region duration, internal start, and name. Logic Pro offsets are not yet independently validated in GarageBand.
- MIDI-005's linked-`MSeq` position scan was inconclusive because the fixture had too few distinct nonzero starts; no scanned payload offset is treated as region start.
- MIDI `0x20` event `+0x14` track identity semantics and whether `+0x04` uses the Logic-derived 34,560/960 PPQ conversion in GarageBand. MIDI-009 rules out treating `+0x14` as a direct arrange-track index in the two inspected fixtures, but it may be an identifier or index into another object table. MIDI-010 found one zero-payload, same-group `Trak` chunk alongside every unique `MSeq` target; whether that group relation identifies an arrange track remains UNKNOWN. The `+0x20` cluster-to-`MSeq` association is HIGH CONFIDENCE in two fixtures; timing semantics still need controlled validation.
- MIDI-004's raw `Trak` chunk-order comparison did not establish a direct mapping for `0x20 +0x14`; repeated chunk groups make ordinal equality insufficient.
- Where the referenced live-loop audio is stored/resolved for this project; the supplied `.band` archive itself contains no audio payload members.
- Audio source references and region-to-media mapping.
- Tempo and time-signature changes, sections, automation, fades, and region gain.
- Whether output metadata and asset lists are complete, stale, or derived caches.
- Which structures differ across GarageBand versions and desktop/mobile projects.

- Semantics of unclassified EvSq event types. EVT-004 inventories aggregate counts and lengths for 50 type bytes outside the five event types with candidate decoders in one fixture, and IDA confirms one representative type byte for each; it does not establish whether these records are MIDI controllers, instrument state, automation, or another structure.
- PROV-001 found GarageBand 2.3.19 in both inspected project archives. User confirms the initial audio-free and later private audio fixtures came from different iPhone models; exact model attribution is kept in local-only notes, and archive product-type strings do not independently establish the mapping. Device-specific serialization effects remain unknown because project contents differ.
- FLP-001 through FLP-006 established an experimental PyFLP-based audio exporter with a Python 3.12+ compatibility shim and save/reparse checks. Whether generated projects load correctly and play the referenced audio in FL Studio 25 remains unverified; PyFLP licensing and supported runtime also remain open.

The next useful inputs are sanitized or private local fixtures created by changing one property at a time: empty project, one track, one note, note pitch/duration/start changes, region position/length changes, tempo/meter changes, and a simple audio region. Do not add private song content to the public source repository.
