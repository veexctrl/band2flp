# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want `band2flp` to be a welcoming, useful place for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Everyone participating in the project is expected to act with respect and consideration.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, patient, and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and evidence without attacking people.
- Respect privacy, consent, and the project’s rules for private recordings and other contributed material.
- Accept feedback and take responsibility for correcting mistakes.
- Keep project discussions focused and welcoming to newcomers.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, and publishing someone’s private information or recordings without consent are not acceptable. Repeated disruptive conduct and other behavior that makes participation unsafe or hostile are also not acceptable.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces, including the repository, issue and pull request discussions, and project-related events or communication. It also applies when someone is representing the project in another space.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainers through GitHub. Do not include sensitive details in a public issue. If GitHub does not provide a private contact option, open a brief issue asking for a private reporting channel without describing the incident.

Maintainers will review reports fairly, respect reporter privacy as far as practical, and take action they consider appropriate to protect participants and the project. Actions may include a warning, removal of content, temporary restrictions, or removal from project spaces. Maintainers should explain enforcement decisions to the people directly involved where doing so is safe and appropriate.

## Attribution

This policy is written for `band2flp` and draws on common open-source community conduct practices.
